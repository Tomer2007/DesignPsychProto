Look Up

A tiny experiment in meeting the people already around you.

How it works

  • Join a group with a shared code (your class, event, or floor).
  • Make a quick profile: name, photo, color.
  • Browse who else is in the group — just names and photos, nothing else.
  • Send a short message — capped at words.
  • The rest of the conversation happens in person. That's the point.
